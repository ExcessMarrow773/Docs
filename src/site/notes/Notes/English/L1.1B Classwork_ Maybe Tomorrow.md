In Charlotte Agell and Ana Ramirez Gonzalez’s Maybe Tomorrow, there is a block, a dark, rough, and heavy block, that is used to represent grief and sadness.

The first example I see of the block as a symbol of grief and sadness is the pictures on the first page. The block looks hand-drawn, almost like it has been gone over and over again with a black crayon, signifying the trauma of losing someone.
