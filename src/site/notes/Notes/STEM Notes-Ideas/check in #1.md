### The Pivot

Choose **one important moment when your team changed direction**.

**Original plan:** What were you trying to do?

**What happened:** What problem, limitation, failure, or new information did you discover?

**The change:** What did your team modify, redesign, reprogram, or approach differently?

**Why:** What evidence or reasoning led your team to make that decision?

_If your team has not made a major change yet, identify something you are currently questioning or considering changing._
