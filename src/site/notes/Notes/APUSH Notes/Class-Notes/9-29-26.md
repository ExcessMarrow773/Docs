## Federalists vs. Democratic-Republicans
 - ### _Federalists_
	 1. Favored the **Constitution**
	 2. Supported **Urban/ City** areas
	 3. **Against** the Bill of Rights
	 4. Wanted a **Strong, central** government
	 5. **British** Alliance
	 6. Ruled by the **wealthy** class

- ### _Democratic-Republicans_
	1. Favored the **Articles of Confederation**
	2. wanted power to stay in the **states** not the Government
	3. emphasis on **country and rural areas**
	4. **Strict** interpretation of the Constitution
	5. **French** Alliance
	6. Ruled but the **People**

## Presidency time
- ### George Washington's Presidency!
	- **Proclamation of Neutrality**: don't support France in war with the British

	- **Whiskey Rebellion**: Uses power of president & military to shut down rebellion over the Whiskey Tax in rural Pennsylvania
	- **Jay's Treaty**: supposed to get British's to stop impressment, instead gets the British to leave some forts in the Louisiana Territory
	- **Hamilton's Financial Plan**: The US will take on all of the state's debt and pay it off over time using federal taxes and tariffs

- ### John Adams' Presidency
	-  **XYZ Affair**: France demanded bribes from the us to start peace talks; US was ***not*** happy
	- **Alien and Sedition Acts**: Passed these laws to silence any french support (& anti-federalist ideology) in the US and remove foreigners from the US (specifically French foreigners)
	- **Kentucky and Virginia Resolutions**: Democratic Republicans declare that states can nullify any law in the US they disagree with and find unconstitutional
		- Direct response to the **Alien and Sedition Acts**

- ### Thomas Jefferson's Presidency
	- Reduces the size of the US military
	- Abolishes the Whiskey Tax
	- **Louisiana Purchase**: set US on the path for Westard expansion and gave the US access to ports on the Mississippi River
	- **Embargo Act**: Banned American ships from trading in all foreign ports. **BIG** failure, just led to economic crash
	- **Barbary Pirates**: pirates are attacking US merchant ships and demanding bribes for safe passage, but Jefferson refuses and war is declared
