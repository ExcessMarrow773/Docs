# 9/15/26

- ## Big Question #7
	- What role does the Enlightenment play in the ideals of the American Revolution?

- ***John Locke***
	- **All individuals are equal** in the sense that they are born with certain "inalienable" natural rights. 
	- **Rights**
		- for
- ***Jean-Jacques Rousseau***
	- The **government** should exist to **protect and support** the **people**
	- when a **government fails to act morally** it has **lost its power** (the people have the right to overthrow it)

- ***Thomas Paine***
	- **Independence** from England & **Creation** of **a Democratic Republic**
	- *Used lots of Biblical references, which helped further rile up colonists who were already upset*

- ***Thomas Jefferson***
	- **All men are created equal and have inalienable right** - life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness
	- The **British Government** is overstepping their authority and **has become oppressive**, as evidenced by the majority of acts they've passed recently
