# The Real Constitution
## Determining Representation
-  **Virginia Plan**
	- Representation based on population
	- Big states like!
- **New Jersey Plan**
	- Equal representation
	- Small states like!
- ## The Great Compromise
	- Two Chamber legislature
	- Representation based on **state population** in House of Representatives
		- Big States Like!
	- **Equal representation** in Senate (every state has two Senators)
		- Small States Like!
- ## Elecortal College
	- These votes **don't** directly determine the outcome of the election
	- The popular votes determine which candidate will be appointed to the Electoral College from each state
	- Slave states have the most power
 - ## Slave Trade Clause
	 - Prohibited the government from saying *anything* about stopping the Transatlantic slave trade
 - ## Fugitive Slave Clause
	 - No Person held to Service of labor in one State **under the Laws thereof**,  escaping into another has to be brought back
	 - Any caught escaped slave from a slave state has to be returned by the free state
		 - even if they are **legally free**

- ## Elastic Clause (Necessary and Proper)
	- Gave congress the power to pass any laws deemed necessary and proper
	- lets Congress claim powers that aren't  listed in the Constitution
		- Congress' power is *stretched*
-  ## Federalists vs. Anti-Federalists
	- **Federalist**: Pro-Constitution
	- **Anti-Federalist**: Anti-Constitution
	- the **Fedaralist Papers** were written to convince state legislatures to accept they newly written Constitution, but it was not ratified until the Anti-Federarlists got their compromise
		- the Bill of RIghts
