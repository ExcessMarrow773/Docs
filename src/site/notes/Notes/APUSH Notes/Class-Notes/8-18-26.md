 ## Columbian Exchange
 - Old world gets 
	 - 
	 - Wheat, rice, soybeans, barley become staple foods in Native cultures
	 - Pigs and Cows become staple meat for Natives
	 - Horses changed the way natives work and their warfare tactics
 - New world gets
	 - 
	 - Potatoes and corn become very popular in Europe, European population explores 
		 - more easily grown food = more people
	 - sugar and Cocoa become in high demand
	 - Tobacco becomes addictive and popular
- Society is transformed by Disease
	- 
	- Tenochtitlan (aztec Capital, Modern Day Mexico city): had estimates of 200,000-400,000 people
		- conquered by Hernan Cortes and 1,000 soldiers b/c smallpox destroyed the army
	- in hispaniola: Arawak and Taino people all but eradicated by disease
	- Aztec and Mayan population before Columbian Exchange: 50 million people
		- After: 3 million
