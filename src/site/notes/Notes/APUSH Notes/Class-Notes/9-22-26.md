- ## Articles of Confederation
	
	 -  ### Weaknesses
		 - 	Congress didn't have the power to tax the colonies, regulate currency, & regulate commerce, and had no Natural currency (every state set their own taxes and had their own currency)
		 - No Executive or Judicial Branch; no way to enforce any acts passed by Congress & no Supreme Court
		 - National Government was unable to combine military forces; almost every state had their own million
	 - ### Strengths
		 - Land Ordinance of 1785
			 - Established policy for surveying and selling Western Land
			 - Expanded Public Education
		 - Northwest ordinance of 1787
			 - set the standard for creating new states (3 stages to Statehood
			 - Prohibited glacier in new territories
			 - Granted limited self-government and set the precedent for westward Expansion
