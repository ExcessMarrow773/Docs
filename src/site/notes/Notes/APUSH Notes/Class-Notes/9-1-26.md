# 9/1/26

- ## Northeast/New England colonies
	- Religion: Puritain/Pilgrim
		- NOT Religiously tolerant 
		- strict, wanted to fully separate from anglican faith, and believed in hard work (Ie. do it yourself)
	- Economy: Exported Fish & Lumber
	- Geography: Rocky soil & Harsh winters = limited agriculture

- ## Middle Colonies (New York, New Jersey, & Pennsylvania)
	- Religion: Quaker
		- Religiously tolerant & diverse
		- Pacifists
		- Had a good relationship with Natives
		- Religiously tolerant 
	- Economy: Exported crops & grains
	- Mixed economy
	- Geography: Great soil = BOOM in agriculture
	- **PORTS ARE IM*PORT*ANT**
- ## Chesapeake Colonies 
	- Religion: Protestant
		- Wanted to reform the church believed in adult baptism, *slightly* less strict
	- Economy: Main export is tobacco
		- All about making that moolah
		- Indentured servitude (at first)
		- Plantation agriculture
	- Geography: Warm environment with good soil

- ## Lower South Colonies (North Carolina, South Carolina, & Georgia)
	- Religion: Protestant
	- Economy: Main export was Rice and Sugar
		- Increased demand for enslaved African labor => Plantation based agriculture
		- Race laws and racial hierarchy
	- Geography: Great soil = Large plantation agriculture
