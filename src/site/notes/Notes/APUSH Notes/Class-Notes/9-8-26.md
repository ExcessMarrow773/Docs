# 9/8/26

# England is increasing their imperial control on the colonies
- ## Seven years war (French and Indian War)
	1. **France and Natives groups have a similar goal**
		1. Limit english colonists moving west
		2. Trade
		
	2. **British Colonists want land** 
		1. Population is growing, and colonists are moving west to claim large tracts of fertile land
		
	3.  **Albany plan of union**
		1. Rep. From various colonies gathered to discuss mutual problems (including defense against the French)
		2. Before this, colonies were operating independently and now have met to discuss bonds and common goals
			- While they ***talked*** about this, some of them actually just don't do it
	- This was where George Washington lead his men into a massacre  
	- **FIGHT!!**
		- French and their allies (Algonquin and Wabanaki)
		- British and their allies (Iroquois Confederacy)
		- British are using impressment (forcing colonists to join the British Navy)
			- Colonists merchants are being grabbed and forced to work on British naval ships
		- British living in Britain don't want to pay for this v expensive war they wont benefit from 
	-  **Treaty of Paris**
		- (1763) - British win, France loses all land except their sugar plantations in the Caribbean

- **Pontiac's Rebellion**
	- Great lakes natives are unhappy with how britain is ruling them, want to prove they are sovereign nations 
	- Algonquian, Iroquoian, Muskogean, and Siouan-speaking Natives unite to fight the british
	- Stalemate ending in Britain recognizing Native autonomy

### Proclamation of 1763
- *Issued by King George III*
	- Prevented American colonists setting on lands acquired during the war.
