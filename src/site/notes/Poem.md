## There are a lot of things I like about Her

Her company
Her laugh

Her hair
Her voice
Her touch

I like the way she makes me feel
I like the way I talk to her
I like the way that she dresses

I love the way her hair smells
I love the things she talks about

I love her
She loves me

She loves herself
But not in a bad way

I'm not sure if I love myself
But I know she loves me

And I can work with that

So like I said
## There are a lot of things I love about Her
